PetSight lets owners scan their pet through a short questionnaire, photos and videos. The answers are analysed by AI and turned into a health report, so owners can keep track of their pet's health over time. Vet care is expensive, and many people want a more accessible way to keep an eye on their animals.

The owner starts by saying what they've noticed. Then the app guides them through three short videos: the face, the way the pet walks, and how it moves when active. The AI reviews each capture, and in under two minutes there's a shareable report with scores, an action plan and a breakdown of the movement.
