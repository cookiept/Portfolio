Find Your Constellation is a quiz that helps designers figure out which creative role fits them best. Six short questions about how they work, what inspires them and how they handle feedback turn into a constellation of their own.

Each result is a cluster (Illustrator, Brand, Editorial or UX/UI) with the paths that might suit them, an expected salary range from junior to lead, the next steps in their journey, courses to grow in that area and masters of the craft to follow.
