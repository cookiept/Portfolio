FBAUL Alumni started as a challenge: design an alumni section for the website of the Faculty of Fine Arts of the University of Lisbon, a place where former students stay connected to the school and to each other.

I designed three pages. The homepage brings the alumni hub next to applying and seeing student work. The Alumni page gathers events, a directory of former classmates, a forum, work by today's alumni, where they work now, a career board and research. The donations page shows how alumni can give back, with funding, mentoring and workshops.
