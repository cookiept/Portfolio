Pinnie is an AI assistant built into Pinterest. Instead of scrolling through endless pins, you tell it what you need, like "I have a date, can you help me find an outfit?", and it breaks the look into pieces: sweater, skirt, boots, accessories. You can also ask about a pin you saw, like the outfit someone is wearing.

Every piece comes with real products and prices, so going from inspiration to buying takes a couple of taps.
